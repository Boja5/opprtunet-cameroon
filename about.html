🌿 Advanced Web Development Project

Built to solve a real national problem

OpportuNet Cameroon is a web platform designed to directly address the youth employment and opportunity crisis identified in Cameroon's national leadership discourse.

The Context

Rooted in a National Call to Action

This project was inspired by challenges identified in Cameroonian national leadership discourse around youth, employment, and the responsible use of information technology.

"

Cameroon's youth face significant barriers to employment and economic participation. Unemployment and underemployment remain critical challenges. Young people lack access to quality training, face social ills and delinquency, suffer from a lack of information and inefficient support systems, and are increasingly vulnerable to digital misuse. These are challenges that technology must help us solve — not worsen.

— Inspired by challenges identified in national leadership discourse on Cameroonian youth and the IT industry
1
Unemployment & Underemployment
A significant portion of Cameroonian youth are either unemployed or working in roles that do not match their skills or aspirations.
2
Lack of Access to Quality Training
Many young Cameroonians are unaware of training programs, bootcamps, and skill-building opportunities that exist across the country.
3
Social Ills & Delinquency
Youth without economic purpose are more vulnerable to social problems. Connecting them to productive opportunities reduces this risk.
4
Lack of Information & Inefficient Support
Opportunities exist but are scattered. Youth lack a single, reliable platform to find what is available in their region.
5
Digital Misuse
Technology is often used unproductively. OpportuNet redirects digital engagement toward career development and economic empowerment.

The Platform

What OpportuNet Cameroon Offers

A fully web-based platform built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript — accessible on any device, in any region of Cameroon.

🔍
Smart Search & Filters
Search by keyword, filter by type, region and sector. Results update instantly as you type.
🗺
Interactive Map of Cameroon
A real interactive map powered by Leaflet.js showing opportunity clusters across all 10 regions.
🏢
Companies & People Directory
Browse company profiles and youth talent profiles. Companies post listings, youth showcase their skills and CV.
👤
User Accounts & CV Generator
Companies manage listings from a dashboard. Youth build profiles that auto-generate a printable CV.
